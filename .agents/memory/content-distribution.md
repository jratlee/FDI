# Content Distribution — Key Rules

**Source:** `docs/content-distribution.md` (full detail)  
**Primary source:** https://ghost.org/resources/content-distribution/

## The rule to always apply
1:1 creation:distribution. Every FDI asset (Field Guide, MarCom OS update, SkillFoundry post) needs equal distribution effort — not just publishing.

## Three-channel taxonomy (use this language with clients)
- **Owned** = site, email list, social profiles, Field Guides — slow to build, durable, compounds. This IS the FDI thesis.
- **Earned/Shared** = citations, shares, mentions — SkillFoundry's Algorithmic Signal gate measures readiness for this.
- **Paid** = ads, sponsorships — deferred; only after owned + earned foundation is established.

## Steph Smith 6-part model → FDI mapping
- Bedrock → Field Guides + email list + llms.txt
- Viral → occasional HN / reddit on new guide launch
- Social → LinkedIn (one platform only)
- Syndication → article → LinkedIn article → quote cards → deck PDF as doc post
- Targeted → Tier 1/2/3 cold-email sequences
- Paid → deferred

## Distribution checklist (every Field Guide)
- LinkedIn long-form article (adapted, not copy-pasted)
- 3–5 LinkedIn posts (individual insights)
- Email to waitlist
- 5–10 direct outreach to named Tier 1 contacts
- Deck PDF as LinkedIn document post

## FDI audience answer (never re-derive)
CMOs + growth marketers at $10M–$500M companies. Platform: LinkedIn. Not Reddit/TikTok/Instagram professionally.
