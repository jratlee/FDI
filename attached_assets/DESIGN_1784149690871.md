## Brand & Style
The brand personality is **aggressive, industrial, and high-velocity**. It is designed for "Growth Cartography"—the act of mapping and operationalizing complex systems. The aesthetic is a refined evolution of **Neo-Brutalism**, characterized by massive, viewport-based typography, high-contrast color blocks, and intentional "glitch" elements like rotated sections and glowing text.

The UI should evoke a sense of urgent authority and technical precision. It rejects soft gradients in favor of raw structural lines, sharp corners, and a "machine-like" functionalism. The target audience is high-level strategic operators who value clarity, speed, and systemic thinking over decorative flourishes.

## Colors
The palette is built on a high-contrast foundation of **Base Dark (#0D0B08)** and high-visibility industrial signals. 

- **Signal Amber (#FFB12B)** is the primary action and focus color, used for primary buttons, borders, and highlighted text.
- **Signal Orange (#FF5E00)** acts as a secondary "warning" or "alert" accent, used for high-impact brand elements and watermarks.
- **Surface Tones** use a range of deeply desaturated umbers and earths to maintain the industrial, "unmapped territory" feel without resorting to pure grays.
- **Typography Colors** are strictly tiered: High-contrast amber/orange for headlines, and **Faded Ink** or **Secondary Fixed** for supporting body copy.

## Typography
Typography is the primary driver of the layout. It uses a **triple-font system**:
1. **Space Grotesk (Display):** Used for massive, viewport-relative headlines that break traditional grid boundaries. These should always be uppercase with tight tracking.
2. **Inter (Body):** Used for functional reading. It provides a neutral, legible counterpoint to the aggressive display type.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Labels/Data):** Used for metadata, technical tags, and structural labels. It reinforces the "system engineering" theme.

**Visual Effects:** Use `text-glow` (amber shadow) on primary display type to simulate a terminal or backlit signal.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a **Vertical Stack** model with intentional **Rotational Disruptions**. 

- **The Grid:** While elements are generally full-width, sections use slight rotations (1deg to -1deg) and scales (105%) to break the standard browser box model.
- **Bleeds:** Typography often "bleeds" off the edge of the screen (e.g., width 120%) to create a sense of scale larger than the device.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing is tight within components but generous between sections to allow the massive typography room to breathe. Use hard borders (2px-4px) rather than padding to define space.

## Elevation & Depth
The system ignores traditional shadows and Z-axis elevation in favor of **Tonal Overlays and High-Contrast Layering**:

- **Stark Overlays:** Depth is created by physically overlapping high-contrast color blocks (e.g., an orange section over a black background).
- **Z-Index Play:** Content often slides under or over "Signal" watermarks which remain fixed or low-opacity in the background.
- **Glassmorphism:** Use only for persistent UI elements (Header/Footer) with `backdrop-blur-md` and 80-95% opacity to maintain context of the content scrolling beneath.
- **The Glow:** Instead of shadows, use "Outer Glows" (box-shadow with primary color and no spread) to highlight active components.

## Shapes
The shape language is **Strictly Geometric and Sharp**. 

- **Corner Radius:** All primary containers, buttons, and input fields should have **0px (Sharp)** corners. 
- **Exceptions:** Very small indicators (like status pips) may use full rounding (pill) to provide a distinct visual contrast from structural elements.
- **Borders:** Use thick, 2px or 4px borders in `signal-amber` or `hairline` to define containers.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Large, full-width blocks with uppercase Space Grotesk type. Primary buttons use `signal-amber` background with `base-dark` text. Hover states invert the colors or switch to `signal-orange`.
- **Cards:** Defined by thick 2px borders rather than shadows. Use `base-dark` or `surface-container` backgrounds. Include a `label-caps` header separated by a horizontal rule.
- **Badges/Chips:** Use `JetBrains Mono` for all badge content. Layout should be rectangular with thin borders.
- **Headers:** Sticky, high-opacity (80%) dark glass with a thick 4px bottom border in `signal-amber`.
- **Watermarks:** Large Material Symbols (Outlined) positioned fixed/center, using 5% opacity and `signal-orange` to act as background texture.
- **Progress/Status Indicators:** Use horizontal bars or simple geometric fills (squares/circles) rather than fluid animations.
