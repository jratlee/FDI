Nostr explained

Why a key,
not a password?

Every platform you've signed up for stores your account in their database. If they go down, get acquired, or ban you, your history goes with them. Nostr works differently.

01 — Your identity

A keypair is your account

Nostr uses standard cryptography (same math as Bitcoin). You hold a private key (nsec…) that proves your identity, and a public key (npub…) that others can see. Nobody grants you an account — you generate it yourself.

02 — Portable

Your identity moves with you

Because your identity is a key you hold — not an account a server holds — you can take it to any Nostr client or relay. Log in with the same nsec anywhere and your identity follows. No "sign in with Google" middleman.

03 — Treat it like a password

Never share your nsec

Your nsec (private key) is everything. Anyone who has it can post as you. Save it in a password manager or write it down offline. If you lose it, there is no recovery — generate a new key and start fresh.
