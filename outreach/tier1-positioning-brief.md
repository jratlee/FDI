# SkillFoundry Tier 1 (US) - Prospecting & Positioning Brief

Goal: find real Tier 1 SkillFoundry buyers in the US, learn how they think, and
use what we learn to sharpen the Tier 2 ($49/mo) and Tier 3 ($2,500/mo) pitch.
This brief plus `tier1-us-prospects.csv` is the working deliverable.

## The chosen beachhead
**AI-forward independent fractional CMOs and solo B2B-SaaS marketing strategists,
US-based.**

Why this niche:
- They sell judgment (positioning, messaging, GTM strategy) but increasingly run
  that judgment inside AI tools every day. That is exactly the person who buys a
  $29 "strategy as code" artifact to test-drive before committing.
- They are reachable. Most run a personal-brand site with a public contact path,
  unlike enterprise CMOs behind gatekeepers.
- They are multipliers. A fractional CMO who likes SkillFoundry can put it in
  front of every client, which is the natural bridge to Tier 2 and Tier 3.
- The category is crowded with generalists, so a sharper "strategy as code"
  workflow is a real edge they will pay to try.

### ICP split
- **Tier 1a (priority): ICP + a fresh trigger.** Publishing about AI in their
  marketing workflow, hiring, launching a productized offer, or newly rebranding
  a solo practice. Strongest first outreach.
- **Tier 1b: ICP, no fresh trigger.** Right profile, monitor for a trigger or
  lead with a value hook.

Everyone in the CSV today is Tier 1b unless the Trigger column is filled. The
Trigger column is deliberately blank where we did not find a real, dated event.
We do not invent triggers.

## Recurring pains this segment feels
1. Every engagement restarts strategy from a blank page; nothing compounds.
2. Their best thinking lives in their head, so it does not scale past their own hours.
3. Clients want proof of rigor before paying, and "trust me" does not close.
4. AI output is generic; they lack a repeatable, branded system to make it sharp.
5. Solo capacity ceiling caps revenue at hours-for-dollars.

SkillFoundry maps to these: the three Signal-to-Value gates turn ad-hoc judgment
into a repeatable, inspectable artifact they can show, reuse, and resell.

## Pitch hypotheses to validate through Tier 1 conversations
- **Tier 1 ($29 one-time):** "Run one Strategic Audit on a real client problem
  and see the gates work." Low-risk credibility test. Hypothesis: this is the
  hook that earns the conversation.
- **Tier 2 ($49/mo):** "Reuse the gates across every client, on tap." Hypothesis:
  the trigger to upgrade is landing a 2nd or 3rd concurrent client.
- **Tier 3 ($2,500/mo):** "Your practice's owned strategy system, white-labelled,
  in front of every client." Hypothesis: the buyer is the fractional CMO who wants
  to productize and stop selling hours. Validate whether $2,500 reads as
  "cheaper than one associate" or "too rich for a solo."

Open questions to bring to calls: What do you already pay for AI tooling? What
would make an AI artifact trustworthy enough to put your name on? Where does your
current workflow break when you add a 3rd client?

## Data honesty notes (read before using the CSV)
- **Emails are inferred patterns, not verified.** Format is `first@domain`, the
  common solo-consultant pattern. Verify each before sending (site contact form,
  MX/catch-all check, or a tool like Hunter).
- **LinkedIn is not scrapable from here.** The LinkedIn Search column is a ready
  Google string; run it to grab the actual profile URL.
- **Geo marked "(confirm)"** needs a quick check; a few directory entries were
  international and were excluded (Angus Jones AU, Michelle Wols ES, and UK-based
  names). Confirm before adding to a US-only push.
- Every row is grounded in a real, public source page (see below).

  Kramer), The Product Marketer (Rory Woodbridge - confirm US), vibeproductmarketing

  task" style posts.

Google strings that work:
- `"fractional CMO" "B2B SaaS" site:*.com -site:linkedin.com` (then filter to solo sites)
- `site:linkedin.com/in "fractional CMO" "SaaS" "United States"`
- `"fractional CMO" (Claude OR ChatGPT OR "AI workflow") B2B SaaS`

Screen each candidate: solo or 1-5 people, US, B2B SaaS/tech focus, and a public
signal they use AI in their work. Score 5 when they already publish about AI in
marketing (that is a warm Tier 1a lead).
