# FDI Outreach Email Sequences

Three-touch sequences for the MarCom Architecture Kit / Transformation Sprint
pitch. Two variants: agency president and in-house CMO. The owner sends these
personally; nothing here is automated.

How to use:
- Replace every `[bracket]` before sending. Never send a bracket.
- Keep specific client stories for live calls. Written copy stays generic.
- Spacing: Touch 1, wait 3-4 business days, Touch 2, wait 4-5 business days,
  Touch 3. Stop after Touch 3 unless they reply.
- Subject lines are suggestions; shorter usually wins.

All claims are framed as risks to assess, not guaranteed outcomes. This
material is general guidance, not legal advice.

---
