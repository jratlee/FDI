# White-Label the Davos Decision Kit for Your Agency

Prepared by: False Dawn Industries
Valid for: 30 days

## The opportunity

The Davos Decision Kit is a live, commercially validated product: a self-serve
decision system for executives weighing whether the World Economic Forum annual
meeting belongs on next January's calendar. It is already built, already
selling, and already tested end to end — checkout, gated download, key email,
license management.

Your agency can offer it under your own brand to your own audience with a
single engagement.

## The product

The Davos Decision Kit: a one-time-purchase decision system, downloadable the
moment someone buys. Four working assets plus an AI advisor setup:

1. **The Go/No-Go Scorecard.** A weighted decision framework producing a
   board-defensible go or no-go recommendation.
2. **The Twelve-Month Runway.** The month-by-month plan working backward from
   the January week: positioning, publishing, access-building, meeting stack.
3. **The Budget Calculator.** A cost worksheet with realistic public-range
   estimates so the buyer prices the trip before committing.
4. **The Visibility Plan Templates.** Meeting-request scripts, briefing-doc
   template, a model high-impact week, and the follow-up system where the
   trip's ROI actually lives.
5. **The AI Advisor setup.** A Start Here prompt sheet and a pre-built Claude
   Skill folder so the kit runs as an interactive advisor in the buyer's own
   AI tools from day one.

## What "white-label" means here

Every document is delivered in editable form with your agency's name, voice,
and brand. Your team reviews and corrects the content before anything ships
under your name. FDI handles the system build, the document architecture, and
the commerce plumbing; your agency supplies the expertise and the brand.

The kit monetizes the audience that is seriously curious but not yet ready for
high-touch advisory. The people who buy a $199 decision kit and then decide to
go are exactly the people who will eventually need a team like yours.

## Suggested buyer pricing

$299 one-time, $199 launch price. Low enough for a corporate card without
approval, high enough to signal senior advice.

## Scope and timeline (2 to 3 weeks)

- Week 1: working session with your team to transfer expertise; FDI
  drafts all assets plus the kit read-me and packaging
- Week 2: your review pass; FDI revises, finalizes launch copy (product page
  copy, launch email, two social posts), and delivers the packaged kit
- Optional week 3: commerce plumbing (see add-on)

All assets delivered in editable form under your brand. Your team's names and
voice; our system and production.

## Investment

**$7,500 fixed** for the product, packaging, and launch copy. Half on
signing, half on delivery.

**Add-on: $2,500** for the commerce plumbing: checkout, gated download, and
email capture wired into your site, tested end to end. This is exactly the
flow the Davos Decision Kit runs today — proven, not speculative.

Alternative structure if preferred: $5,000 fixed plus 20 percent of kit
revenue for 12 months.

## What this is not

No claim of WEF affiliation in any asset; all cost figures framed as public
ranges. No open-ended consulting tail: the engagement ends at delivery, and
the kit is yours outright.
