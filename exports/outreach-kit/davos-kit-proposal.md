# Proposal: The Davos Decision Kit

Prepared for: Heather Kernahan, The Content Bureau
Prepared by: False Dawn Industries
Valid for: 30 days

## The gap

Your team's Davos practice has two doors today. The free door: the Davos
Curious briefing, generous and effective, but it leaves the attendee with
notes, not a system. The big door: high-touch advisory, the right answer for
committed clients but a five-figure first step. Between them is nothing, and
that gap is where most of your audience lives: executives who are seriously
curious but not yet ready to engage a team.

## The product

The Davos Decision Kit: a self-serve, one-time-purchase decision system,
downloadable the moment someone buys. Four working assets built from your
team's expertise:

1. **The Go/No-Go Scorecard.** A weighted decision framework producing a
   board-defensible go or no-go recommendation.
2. **The Twelve-Month Runway.** The month-by-month plan working backward from
   the January week: positioning, publishing, access-building, meeting stack.
3. **The Budget Calculator.** A cost worksheet with realistic public-range
   estimates so the buyer prices the trip before committing.
4. **The Visibility Plan Templates.** Meeting-request scripts, briefing-doc
   template, a model high-impact week, and the follow-up system where the
   trip's ROI actually lives.

Every asset ends at the same next step: book a strategy session with your
team. The kit monetizes the curious who never convert, qualifies the ones who
will, and hands you a warm, pre-educated pipeline. The people who buy a $199
decision kit and then decide to go are exactly the people who need advisory.

## Suggested buyer pricing

$299 one-time, $199 launch price. Low enough for a corporate card without
approval, high enough to signal senior advice. It sits cleanly between the
free briefing and advisory, and it never competes with either.

## Scope and timeline (2 to 3 weeks)

- Week 1: working session with your team to transfer the expertise; FDI
  drafts all four assets plus the kit read-me and packaging
- Week 2: your review pass; FDI revises, finalizes launch copy (product page
  copy, launch email, two social posts), and delivers the packaged kit
- Optional week 3: commerce plumbing (see add-on)

All assets delivered in editable form under your brand. Your team's names and
voice; our system and production.

## Investment

**$7,500 fixed** for the product, packaging, and launch copy. Half on
signing, half on delivery.

**Add-on: $2,500** for the commerce plumbing: checkout, gated download, and
email capture wired into your site, tested end to end.

Alternative structure if preferred: $5,000 fixed plus 20 percent of kit
revenue for 12 months.

## What this is not

No claim of WEF affiliation in any asset; all cost figures framed as public
ranges. No open-ended consulting tail: the engagement ends at delivery, and
the kit is yours outright.
