# FDI Discovery Call Guide

A 30-minute structure for the first live call with an agency president or
CMO. Goal: qualify the fit, map their situation to the today/tomorrow service
map, and leave with a concrete next step (Sprint proposal, Governance Risk
Audit, or a polite no).

Keep specific client stories for this call; they never go in writing.

## Before the call (5 minutes of prep)

- Skim their site and LinkedIn: org size, service lines, any public AI
  positioning.
- Pick the wedge that fits: deskilling (talent pipeline) for presidents,
  budget defense and governance for CMOs.
- Know your two offers cold: Sprint (fixed 4 weeks, $10,000) and the
  Governance Risk Audit ($1,500 to $2,500) as the lighter entry.

## Call structure

### 1. Frame (2 minutes)

"Thanks for the time. My goal today is to understand how your team actually
uses AI right now, and whether the structural stuff we do is relevant. If it
is not, I will say so."

### 2. Discovery questions (12 minutes)

Current state (today):
- How is AI showing up in the team's day-to-day work right now?
- Who decided which tools you use? Is there a list, or did it accumulate?
- What happens when a junior produces AI-assisted work? Who reviews it, and
  against what standard?
- Have legal or brand raised concerns about AI use yet? What happened?

Direction (tomorrow):
- Where do your next senior people come from if the production work is
  automated?
- If the CEO or board asked "what is our AI operating model", what would you
  show them today?
- What would you cut from the stack tomorrow if you had cover to do it?

Listen for the four buying signals:
1. Tool sprawl with no owner (Use/Compose/Build fit).
2. No review standard for AI-assisted output (Riverbank fit).
3. Anxiety about the talent pipeline (Hourglass fit).
4. A budget or board conversation coming (Audit to Kill fit).

### 3. Map to the service ladder (8 minutes)

Present as a today/tomorrow map, not a menu:

- **Today, self-serve:** MarCom OS. Playbook, org
  blueprint, calculator, governance templates. For teams that will do the
  work themselves.
- **Today, guided:** the Governance Risk Audit ($1,500 to $2,500). A
  structured review of where current AI use exposes them, with the fee
  credited toward the Sprint if they upgrade within 90 days.
- **Tomorrow, installed:** the Transformation Sprint. Fixed four weeks,
  fixed $10,000, ends with the structure installed and the team briefed.
- **Ongoing:** the Architecture Partner retainer, only if they raise it.

Rule: recommend the smallest thing that genuinely fits. It builds trust and
the audit converts.

### 4. Close (5 minutes)

Strong fit for the Sprint:
"Based on what you described, the Sprint is the right shape. I will send a
one-page proposal with the four-week plan and the fixed price. If it reads
right, we pick a start date."

Interested but hesitant:
"The lighter way in is the Governance Risk Audit. Fixed fee, two weeks,
you get a written read on where your current AI use is exposed. If you go on
to the Sprint within 90 days, the audit fee is credited."

Not a fit:
"Honestly, I do not think you need us yet. Take the free starter pack, and
if [specific trigger they mentioned] happens, that is the moment to talk."

Always end with a date: either a proposal delivery date or a follow-up date.

### 5. After the call (same day)

- Log the target's stage and next action in the pipeline tracker.
- Send the promised artifact (proposal, audit outline, or starter pack)
  within 24 hours.

## Objection notes

- "We already use AI heavily." Usage is not structure. Ask who owns the
  review standard; the silence usually makes the point.
- "Can you guarantee savings?" No. The audit identifies candidates to cut;
  what to act on stays their call. Softened claims only.
- "Why not a big consultancy?" Fixed scope, fixed price, four weeks, and
  the artifacts stay theirs. No open-ended engagement.

*General guidance for the seller, not legal advice.*
