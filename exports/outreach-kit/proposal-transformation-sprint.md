## The engagement: Transformation Sprint

A fixed four-week engagement that installs an AI operating structure in your
marketing organization. Fixed scope, fixed price, fixed calendar. It ends
with the structure installed and your team briefed, not with a
recommendations deck.

### Week by week

**Week 1: Map.** Inventory of current tools, workflows, and review practices.
Interviews with [N] team leads. Output: the current-state map and the Audit
to Kill candidate list.

**Week 2: Design.** The target Hourglass org shape for your team, the
Use/Compose/Build ruling on every tool in the stack, and the Riverbank
governance guardrails adapted to your legal and brand constraints. Output:
the target-state blueprint, reviewed with you before anything changes.

**Week 3: Install.** Governance templates adapted and adopted, review
standards for AI-assisted work put in place, kill list decisions executed by
your team with our support. Output: the operating structure, live.

**Week 4: Brief and hand over.** Leadership briefing, team walkthrough, and
the complete artifact set handed over. Output: your team runs the structure
without us.

### What you receive

- Current-state map and Audit to Kill candidate list
- Target Hourglass org blueprint for your team
- Use/Compose/Build ruling on your full tool stack
- Riverbank governance templates, adapted and adopted
- Leadership briefing and team walkthrough
- All artifacts in editable form; they are yours

### What this is not

No tool resale, no headcount recommendations delivered over your head, no
open-ended consulting tail. The Sprint ends on [end date].

## Investment

**$10,000, fixed.** Half on signing, half at the Week 2 blueprint review.
Scheduling: kickoff on [date], handover on [date].

## A lighter first step, if preferred

If a four-week commitment is more than the moment calls for, the
**Governance Risk Audit** ($1,500 to $2,500 depending on team size) is a
two-week structured review of where current AI use may expose the
organization, delivered as a written report. The full audit fee is credited
toward the Sprint if you proceed within 90 days.
