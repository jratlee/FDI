# 06 · Feedback Loop & Thesis Validation

Every session must produce structured signal on one question: **does the
owned-marketing-systems thesis actually resonate with the people whose job is
strategy?** This is a built-in instrument, run the same way every time (Beat 7),
so signal is comparable across sessions and cities.

## What we are trying to learn

Three validation targets, each tied to a claim in *Build the Machine, Not the Ad*:

1. **Pain is real:** execution is commoditized and their strategy is the moat.
2. **The gap exists:** they do not currently own a corpus / graph / interface a
   machine can cite, and they feel that gap.
3. **The reframe converts:** after building one owned answer object, "own the
   system" is *more* compelling than "buy more reach," and compelling enough to act.

## Instrument 1 — The thesis dot-vote (live, 3 min)

A wall poster with a single spectrum. Attendees place one sticky dot.

> **"Where does your durable advantage live in 2026?"**
> `Reach and execution you buy` ————————————— `Systems and identity you own`

- **Entry vote:** put the poster up during arrival (Beat 1) and ask everyone to
  dot it before the cold open. Use **color 1**.
- **Exit vote:** same poster, everyone re-dots at close (Beat 7). Use **color 2**.
- **The signal:** the shift from color-1 to color-2 dots toward the "own" end is
  the thesis lift for that session. Photograph the poster; it is both data and
  social proof for the next session's open.

## Instrument 2 — The exit card (written, 5 min)

A quarter-page card, collected at the door. Five prompts, ~2 minutes to fill. Keep
it short or completion drops.

> **The Cartographers' Table · Exit Card · Session ___**
>
> 1. **Pain check (1 to 5):** "In my work, execution is becoming commoditized and
>    my strategy is my real moat." `1 disagree ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ 5 agree`
> 2. **Gap check (yes / no):** "Today, I own a corpus, graph, or interface that a
>    machine could cite and verify." `Yes ○   No ○`
> 3. **Reframe check (1 to 5):** "After tonight, 'own the system' beats 'buy more
>    reach' for where I put my next effort." `1 ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ 5`
> 4. **The moment it clicked:** one line. ________________________
> 5. **The moment I doubted it:** one line (be honest, this is the most useful
>    box). ________________________
>
> Optional: **What would make you pay for the repeatable version?** ____________

Questions 1 to 3 map one-to-one to the three validation targets and give a numeric
trend across sessions. Questions 4 and 5 surface the language attendees actually
use (gold for refining the pitch and the Field Guides) and the live objections
(the doubt box is where the thesis gets stress-tested).

## Instrument 3 — The reply-back (Day 1, async)

Follow-up Touch 2 (see `05`) asks the single sharpest question again by email:

> "One line back: after building your answer object, what is the strongest reason
> the owned-systems thesis is *right*, and the strongest reason it might be *wrong*
> for your practice?"

Replies capture considered second-day reactions, which are more honest than
in-room enthusiasm. They also re-open a conversation that routes to the funnel.

## Lightweight capture method (no new code)

Keep it to a spreadsheet. One row per attendee per session, so trends are
queryable without building anything.

**Columns:**

| Column | Source |
|---|---|
| `session` | which session (e.g., `aggregated`) |
| `date` | session date |
| `attendee_count` | headcount |
| `pain_1to5` | exit card Q1 (record per attendee, or the session mean) |
| `gap_yes_no` | exit card Q2 |
| `reframe_1to5` | exit card Q3 |
| `clicked_line` | exit card Q4 (verbatim) |
| `doubted_line` | exit card Q5 (verbatim) |
| `pay_trigger` | exit card optional |
| `entry_dots` / `exit_dots` | dot-vote tallies (own-end count) |
| `source_tag` | the `workshop-*` tag for funnel join (see `05`) |

Transcribe exit cards the same night while the room is fresh. The dot-vote is a
single tally of dots past the midpoint, entry vs. exit.

**Note on tooling:** this is deliberately a spreadsheet, not a new site feature.
Building an in-product feedback form is out of scope; the `pay_trigger` and verbatim
columns are the highest-value inputs to the SkillFoundry roadmap and the next
Field Guide, so protect the time to capture them.

## Reading the signal (decision rules)

- **Green (thesis validated for this segment):** mean `reframe_1to5` ≥ 4, exit
  dots shift ≥ 20 points toward "own" vs. entry, and ≥ 3 funnel actions per
  session within 10 days (from `05`). Keep the format, scale cadence and cities.
- **Yellow (resonates but does not convert):** high `reframe_1to5` but few funnel
  actions. The story lands, the offer or the bridge does not. Rework Beat 6 and
  the Day-7 offer touch, not the thesis.
- **Red (thesis does not land):** flat or negative reframe shift and recurring
  themes in the doubt box. Do not paper over it. Feed the specific objections back
  into the Field Guide argument and the audience assumptions in `01`. This is the
  workshop doing its most valuable job: telling FDI the truth early.

## Close the loop each session

Open every session (Beat 2 or the intro) by showing the prior session's dot-vote
photo and one verbatim "clicked" line. This proves the room is a living scoreboard
(a return force, see `02`) and signals that FDI actually listens to the feedback it
collects.
