---

## How to use this file

1. **Pick the right variant for each prospect** (see the Variant Selector and Per-Prospect Notes at the end).
2. **Swap the `[PERSONALIZATION]` tokens** with the prospect-specific lines from the notes.
3. **Use the "Why Now" column from the CSV verbatim or lightly adapted** as the opening sentence of Touch 1 — it was written for exactly this purpose.
4. **Verify each email address before sending.** Most are noted as unverified patterns.
5. Send touches 1–4 at the cadence shown. Stop the sequence the moment a reply arrives.

# Sequence Cadence Summary

| Touch | Timing | Goal | CTA |
|-------|--------|------|-----|
| T1 | Day 0 | Hook on their specific trigger + one-line value prop | "Worth a look?" / "Happy to demo" |
| T2 | Day 4–5 | Go deeper on one concrete pain (usually P2 — trapped strategy) | "$29 to try, or 15-min demo" |
| T3 | Day 10–12 | Switch pain angle (usually to P4 — credibility/verifiability) | "$29 link, no call required" |
| T4 | Day 17–19 | Graceful exit, leave door open | Bare link, no ask |

**Stop the sequence immediately on any reply — positive or negative.**
A "not now" reply is worth a warm follow-up in 60–90 days.
A negative reply removes them from the sequence permanently.

---

## Sending notes

- **Personalize Touch 1 only.** Touches 2–4 are near-universal; the only personalization needed is the first name and, optionally, a callback to something you said in T1.
- **Subject-line test:** For prospects with large audiences (Indig, Poyar, Kramer, Penn, Noel Block), A/B the "newsletter-worthy" vs. "personal utility" subjects. The personal-utility subjects tend to outperform for practitioners who get a lot of "can you mention us" mail.
- **Verified emails first:** Dale Bertrand (dale@fireandspark.com) and Brennon Bynum (brennon@brennonbynumcmo.com) are the only Tier 1a/1b addresses marked verified. Run an email-verification pass (Hunter, NeverBounce, or similar) on the unverified patterns before sequencing.
- **Plain text only.** This audience is sophisticated about cold email. No HTML templates, no tracking pixels on first send, no "sent from my iPhone" tricks. Clean plain text.
