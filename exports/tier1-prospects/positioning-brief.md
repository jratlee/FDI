# Tier 1 Positioning Brief — SkillFoundry (US)

*Companion to `tier1-us-prospects.csv`. Prepared as of July 2026. All prospects
are real, web-verifiable US individuals/practices sourced only from free web
research. Emails are noted as likely patterns (marked unverified) unless a public
address was found; nothing is fabricated.*

---

## (a) The chosen beachhead niche — and why it beat the alternatives

**Chosen niche: AI-forward fractional CMOs and independent B2B marketing/growth
strategists serving SaaS & tech companies (US, solo or 1–15 person practice).**

This confirms the recommended beachhead. It won over the two strongest
alternatives for four reasons:

1. **They already live in AI tooling.** The list is dominated by people who
   publicly build their practice around AI search (GEO/AEO), AI-native GTM, and
   AI agents (e.g., Kevin Indig, Eli Schwartz, Dale Bertrand, Lillian Pierson,
   Taylor Scher, Christopher Penn). "Strategy as code" over the open Model
   Context Protocol lands with zero education for this group.
2. **They personally feel SkillFoundry's core pain.** As solo operators they *are*
   the strategic oversight layer for every client. When execution gets automated,
   their differentiation is the quality and repeatability of their strategy —
   exactly what a verifiable, source-authority-graded system reinforces.
3. **They buy $29 tools instantly.** No procurement, no legal, no seat
   negotiation. A single practitioner expenses a $29 one-time license the same
   afternoon they see the demo.
4. **They are referral nodes into Tier 2 and Tier 3.** Nearly every name runs a
   newsletter, podcast, community, or heavy LinkedIn presence (Emily Kramer, Kyle
   Poyar, Dave Gerhardt, Sarah Noel Block, Tas Bober). One convinced Tier 1 user
   becomes distribution into the in-house (Tier 2) and enterprise/agency (Tier 3)
   motions.

**Alternative 1 — boutique PR/comms shops:** real Tier 1 buyers, but weaker AI
signal and a looser fit with an MCP/"signal-as-code" story. Kept as a secondary
segment, not the beachhead.

**Alternative 2 — independent content/SEO consultants:** strong AI-search
overlap, but many are narrowly tactical (rankings/traffic) rather than owners of
*strategy*. We folded the best of them in where they explicitly sell strategy
or AEO (Scher, Schwartz, Indig, Bertrand), rather than making them the core.

**Negative signals used to exclude / down-rank:** non-US location (April Dunford,
Kasey Bayne, Ritika Mehta, Alon Even, Michelle Wols, and others were dropped);
50+ person agencies (Tuff, Olivine, Orbit Media) that behave like Tier 2/3
buyers; and in-house/employed roles rather than independent practitioners.

## (b) The 3–4 recurring pains / triggers across the list

1. **"Execution is commoditized; my strategy is the only moat."** Repeated
   across fractional CMOs and positioning shops (Fletch, Punchy, Forget The
   Funnel). AI does the doing; they are paid for judgment — and they have no way
   to make that judgment *repeatable or provable* across clients.
2. **Strategy is trapped in their head (or a slide deck).** Solo operators
   re-build the same frameworks per engagement. A tool that encodes strategy once
   as verifiable, reusable code is direct leverage on billable time.
3. **AI search is rewriting the game and clients are panicking.** GEO/AEO,
   source authority, and "will an LLM cite us?" show up constantly (Indig,
   Schwartz, Bertrand, Scher, King, Durrani). Source-authority grading is an
   active, dated pain, not a nice-to-have.
4. **Credibility / verifiability pressure.** These practitioners stake their
   reputation on advice. A system that grades source authority and produces an
   auditable trail lets them defend recommendations to clients.

*Trigger note for outreach prioritization:* Tier 1a (14 prospects) are those with
a **recurring public cadence** (weekly newsletter/podcast or near-daily posting)
that guarantees a signal within the last 30 days — the fastest to reach with a
timely hook. Tier 1b (29) are strong ICP fits without a dated recent event.

## (c) Mapping Tier 1 insights into Tier 2 and Tier 3 pitch hypotheses

### Tier 2 — in-house content / comms / growth teams ($49/mo subscription)
- **Hypothesis 1 (from pain #2):** In-house teams have the same "strategy trapped
  in one senior head" problem at *team* scale. Pitch SkillFoundry as the shared,
  version-controlled strategy layer that keeps a whole team's output on-strategy —
  the recurring seat-based value a one-time license can't deliver.
- **Hypothesis 2 (from pain #3):** Sell the AEO/source-authority engine as an
  ongoing monitoring subscription ("is our category still being cited correctly
  by LLMs?"), which is inherently continuous, not one-time.
- **Hypothesis 3 (referral path):** Land via the Tier 1 fractional CMO *already
  embedded* in the account — they become the internal champion who upgrades their
  client's team to Tier 2.

### Tier 3 — enterprise comms/PR departments & agencies wanting advisory ($2,500/mo retainer)
- **Hypothesis 1 (from pain #4):** Enterprises need governance and auditability
  across many contributors and regulated claims. Position Tier 3 as the
  verifiable, access-controlled strategy system of record with human advisory —
  the "verifiable MCP interface + oversight" that a compliance-sensitive org buys.
- **Hypothesis 2 (from the referral insight):** The larger agencies down-ranked
  out of Tier 1 (iPullRank, Impactable, Orbit Media, Tuff, Olivine) are ideal
  Tier 3 targets — they want to *offer* strategy-as-code to their own clients and
  need the advisory/retainer relationship, not a $29 tool.
- **Hypothesis 3 (from pain #1 at scale):** Sell Tier 3 as the way an enterprise
  turns "our agency's strategy" into an owned, portable, verifiable asset —
  reducing agency lock-in — which is exactly the owned-systems thesis FDI already
  markets.

## How to use this file

recurring trigger), then Tier 1b. The **Why Now** column is written to be usable
verbatim as the opening line of a cold email. Verify each email pattern before
sending (out of scope here); several are marked unverified by design.
