# The Use, Compose, Build Starter Worksheet
### Ten minutes to the right posture on any AI or MarTech decision

**Free edition, from MarCom OS. As of 2026.**

---

## The three postures

- **USE**: rent it off the shelf. Right when the capability is a commodity and using it creates no durable advantage.
- **COMPOSE**: assemble it from existing APIs and open standards with thin glue. Right when the advantage is in your workflow, not the model.
- **BUILD**: create it from scratch. Right almost never, and only for capability inseparable from unique IP you already own.

The expensive mistake is Building what should be Composed and Composing what should be Used.

## Score it

For the capability on the table, answer each question and circle the posture it points at. Most circles wins; ties go to the cheaper posture (Use beats Compose beats Build).

| # | Question | Points to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do three or more credible vendors sell this today? | Yes: USE. Pieces exist but not the whole: COMPOSE. Nobody sells it: keep reading |
| 2 | If a competitor used the identical tool, would your output be indistinguishable? | Yes: USE. No, because our workflow differs: COMPOSE. No, because our data or IP differs: BUILD candidate |
| 3 | Does it touch your unique narrative or proprietary data corpus directly? | No: USE. Indirectly: COMPOSE. Directly and centrally: BUILD candidate |
| 4 | Could one technical generalist maintain the glue code? | Yes: COMPOSE is affordable. You would need to hire a team: BUILD is probably a trap |
| 5 | If the vendor tripled pricing tomorrow, how bad is it? | Shrug: USE. Painful but we own the arrangement: COMPOSE. Existential: rethink the dependency |

## The honest-accounting gut check

Before any BUILD verdict stands, write down: engineering cost times two (every internal estimate doubles), plus roughly 20 percent of that per year forever in maintenance, plus what those people would have billed doing client work, plus this: the frontier models improve every quarter and your proprietary build does not.

If the number still makes sense, you may genuinely have a BUILD case. They exist. They are rare.

## Three fast examples

| Capability | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Report and QBR drafting | USE |
| AI content generation with your own governance gates in front | COMPOSE |
| "Our own private ChatGPT for the agency" | Fails the gut check every year it is proposed |
