# LinkedIn Thesis Article · Image Placement Guide

This package contains the article and four images. LinkedIn's article editor does not
read Markdown, so upload each image manually at the spot described below. The article
Markdown (`linkedin-thesis-article.md`) already has the images embedded in these exact
positions if you preview it anywhere that renders Markdown.

## Where each image goes

### 1. `li-article-header-1200x627.png` — COVER IMAGE
Set as the article's title/cover image (the banner at the very top, above the headline
"Build the Machine, Not the Ad"). In the LinkedIn article editor this is the large
"add a cover image" slot.

### 2. `viz-identity-bridge-1200x620.png` — after the Field Guide diagnosis paragraph
Insert immediately AFTER the paragraph that ends:
> "...how humans have coordinated around shared belief for roughly 70,000 years."
and BEFORE the line:
> "That's the map. Here is the terrain I've been building on..."

Suggested caption:
> Belief lives in culture, trust lives in experience, and persistent identity is the span that carries one across to the other.

### 3. `viz-two-builds-1200x700.png` — after the Talk to NYC paragraph
Insert immediately AFTER the paragraph that ends:
> "...query it directly, as a tool inside their own workflows."
and BEFORE the line:
> "Those two builds aren't a portfolio..."

Suggested caption:
> Same goal from two directions: Pile puts honesty in a database constraint, Talk to NYC puts shared reality in a graph you can check.

### 4. `viz-three-markets-1200x620.png` — after the Autonomous paragraph
Insert immediately AFTER the paragraph that ends:
> "...never let data become a command."
and BEFORE the line:
> "Here is the urgency..."

Suggested caption:
> Three shifts, happening at once, each answered by the same move: own the identity, the corpus, and the interface instead of renting reach.

## Posting tips
- LinkedIn articles support inline hyperlinks. When you paste the text, link the source
  mentions (Stratechery, Fortune, Dentsu) to boost credibility and dwell time.
- Add the images as you reach each insertion point rather than all at the end; the editor
  places an image wherever your cursor is.
- Alt text is already written into each Markdown image tag if you need it for accessibility.
