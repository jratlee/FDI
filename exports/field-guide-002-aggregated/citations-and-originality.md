## Claim-by-claim findings

### 1. "Three companies are forecast to take 62.3% of all worldwide digital ad spending in 2026"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — eMarketer forecast, labeled as such.**
eMarketer's April 2026 press release projects Meta at $243.46B (26.8% share), Google at $239.54B (26.4%), Amazon at roughly $82.07B (~9%), totalling 62.3%.
**Source:** [eMarketer · Meta to Surpass Google in Digital Ad Revenues for First Time Ever, Apr 2026](https://www.emarketer.com/press-releases/meta-to-surpass-google-in-digital-ad-revenues-for-first-time-ever/)

### 2. "average organic reach for a Facebook page post at about 1.65% of followers. Instagram sits near 3.50%"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — Socialinsider 2025 benchmark data.**
Socialinsider's 2025 social media reach benchmarks document these figures as averages across page types, both declining year over year.
**Source:** [Socialinsider · Social Media Reach: Statistics For 2025](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-reach/)

### 3. "across 31,059 posts with traffic-source data, 85.1% of views arrived through the algorithmic For You page and roughly 0.3% came from the feed of people who actually chose to follow the account"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — quso.ai 31,059-post traffic-source study.**
quso.ai's TikTok algorithm research documents the For You page at 85.1% of views, Following feed at ~0.3%, search at ~1.1%, profile visits at ~2%.
**Source:** [quso.ai · How the TikTok Algorithm Works: 85% Comes from the FYP](https://quso.ai/research/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works)

### 4. "Meta's own full-year 2025 results report the average price per ad up 9% while impressions grew 12%"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — Meta primary earnings release.**
Meta's Q4 and full-year 2025 results: FY ad revenue $196.175B (+22% YoY); average price per ad +9% FY (+6% Q4); ad impressions +12% FY (+18% Q4).
**Source:** [Meta · Q4 and Full-Year 2025 Results (PR Newswire), Jan 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/meta-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results-302673127.html)

### 5. "Tinuiti's Q3 2025 benchmark of roughly $4 billion in managed media … Facebook CPMs fell 6% … Google search CPCs dipped 1% … TikTok CPMs dropped 22%"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — Tinuiti primary benchmark report.**
Tinuiti's Q3 2025 Digital Ads Benchmark Report covers ~$4B in managed media: Meta spend +14% YoY, impressions +16%, CPM -2%; Facebook CPM -6% on +15% impressions (Reels dilution); Google search spend +10%, CPC -1% (Amazon temporarily exiting shopping auctions); TikTok spend -4%, CPM -22% (U.S. regulatory uncertainty).
**Source:** [Tinuiti · Digital Ads Benchmark Report Q3 2025, Oct 2025](https://tinuiti.com/research-insights/research/digital-ads-benchmark-report-q3-2025/)

### 6. "Meta's engineering blog describes Andromeda … selecting from tens of millions of ad candidates for every impression opportunity"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — Engineering at Meta primary post.**
Meta's engineering blog describes Andromeda as the retrieval system behind Advantage+ automation, narrowing tens of millions of ad candidates to a few thousand per impression using deep neural networks co-designed with NVIDIA and MTIA.
**Source:** [Engineering at Meta · Meta Andromeda: Supercharging Advantage+ automation, Dec 2024](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/12/02/production-engineering/meta-andromeda-advantage-automation-next-gen-personalized-ads-retrieval-engine/)

### 7. Zuckerberg "connect to your bank account … read the results that we spit out" quote
**Verdict: VERIFIED — primary Stratechery interview; full quote via Mi3.**
Zuckerberg made this statement in his May 1, 2025 interview with Ben Thompson on Stratechery (partially paywalled). The full quote is reproduced by Mi3.
**Sources:**
- [Stratechery (Ben Thompson) · An Interview with Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg About AI and the Evolution of Social Media, May 2025](https://stratechery.com/2025/an-interview-with-meta-ceo-mark-zuckerberg-about-ai-and-the-evolution-of-social-media/)
- [Mi3 · Zuckerberg calls end of creative agencies, humans for results-hunting AI, May 2025](https://www.mi-3.com.au/05-05-2025/zuckerberg-calls-end-creative-agencies-humans-results-hunting-ai)

### 8. "Pew Research, watching the real browsing behavior of 900 U.S. adults across 68,879 Google searches … 58% hit an AI-generated summary … only about 1% clicked a source cited inside the summary"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — Tier 1 Pew observed-behavior study.**
Pew Research Center's July 2025 study analyzed actual browsing data (not self-report) from 900 U.S. adults across 68,879 Google searches in March 2025. Key findings: 58% encountered at least one AI Overview; users were less likely to click any link when a summary appeared; approximately 1% clicked a cited source link inside an AI Overview.
**Source:** [Pew Research Center · Google users are less likely to click on links when an AI summary appears in the results, Jul 22, 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)

### 9. "Bain found roughly 80% of search users now lean on AI summaries for at least 40% of their searches, that about 60% of searches end with no click to any website, and estimates the shift is cutting organic web traffic by 15% to 25%"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — Bain & Company primary research.**
Bain's February 2025 research found ~80% of search users rely on AI summaries for at least 40% of their searches; ~60% of searches end without progressing to a website; organic web traffic estimated down 15–25% as a result.
**Source:** [Bain & Company · Goodbye Clicks, Hello AI: Zero-Click Search Redefines Marketing, Feb 2025](https://www.bain.com/insights/goodbye-clicks-hello-ai-zero-click-search-redefines-marketing/)

### 10. "Chartbeat data published by the Reuters Institute shows global publisher traffic from Google down roughly a third in the year to November 2025"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — Reuters Institute / Chartbeat via Press Gazette.**
Chartbeat data in the Reuters Institute's Journalism and Technology Trends and Predictions 2026 report documents global publisher traffic from Google fell roughly one-third (U.S. organic referrals down ~38%) in the year to November 2025.
**Sources:**
- [Reuters Institute · Journalism and Technology Trends and Predictions 2026 (primary report)](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/journalism-media-and-technology-trends-and-predictions-2026)
- [Press Gazette · Global publisher Google traffic dropped by a third in 2025, Jan 2026](https://pressgazette.co.uk/media-audience-and-business-data/google-traffic-down-2025-trends-report-2026/)

### 11. "Chegg sued Google"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — CNBC primary report.**
Chegg filed suit against Google in February 2025, stating it depended on Google referrals for a large share of revenue and alleging AI Overviews materially hurt its traffic. Chegg's stock fell to near $1 and its market cap fell below $200M.
**Source:** [CNBC · Chegg sues Google for hurting traffic with AI as it considers strategic alternatives, Feb 24, 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/02/24/chegg-sues-google-for-hurting-traffic-as-it-considers-alternatives.html)

### 12. "Penske Media, publisher of Rolling Stone and Variety, sued too, alleging a coercive bargain"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — TechCrunch and Forbes primary reports.**
Penske Media (Rolling Stone, Variety, Billboard) sued Google in September 2025, alleging publishers face a "coercive bargain": allow content into AI Overviews or accept worse organic rankings.
**Sources:**
- [TechCrunch · Rolling Stone owner Penske Media sues Google over AI summaries, Sep 14, 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/14/rolling-stone-owner-penske-media-sues-google-over-ai-summaries/)

**Verdict: BORROWED FRAMEWORK. ATTRIBUTED.**
Aggregation Theory is Ben Thompson's, first published on Stratechery in 2015. The article names him as originator and frames the AI answer layer as an extension of his theory, not a competing claim.

### 14. "roughly 85% by AirOps's count, originate from third-party pages, not your own domain"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — AirOps 2026 State of AI Search report.**
AirOps's 2026 State of AI Search report (with Kevin Indig) documents that ~85% of brand mentions in AI answers come from third-party pages, not owned domains; ~48% from community platforms (Reddit, YouTube).
**Source:** [AirOps · The 2026 State of AI Search: How Modern Brands Stay Visible, Dec 2025](https://www.airops.com/report/the-2026-state-of-ai-search)

### 15. "in controlled tests, when competing products had identical specs, the well-known brand was recommended every single time"
**Verdict: VERIFIED — arXiv preprint (June 2026); not yet peer-reviewed.**
"Incumbent Advantage" (Chu & Hou, arXiv:2606.17443, Jun 16, 2026) tested GPT-4o-mini, Claude Sonnet, and Gemini 3 Flash. With identical product specifications, well-known incumbents were recommended 100% of the time. The incumbency bias disappeared once a competitor held even a ~0.1-star rating advantage.
**Source:** [arXiv · Incumbent Advantage: Brand Bias and Cognitive Manipulation Dynamics in LLM Recommendation Systems (Chu & Hou), Jun 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17443)
