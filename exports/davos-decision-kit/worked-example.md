# Worked Example: Running the Davos Decision Kit End to End

**This is a fictional example.** "Solvra" and every person named here are
invented for illustration. Any resemblance to a real company or person is
coincidental. All figures are public-range estimates, not quotes. This is
general guidance, not legal or financial advice. The kit is independent and
not affiliated with or endorsed by the World Economic Forum.

## The buyer

Solvra is a fictional Series C climate-fintech (carbon-credit settlement
rails, ~180 people, HQ Amsterdam). CEO Maren Vold is weighing Davos week,
January 2027. A Series D raise is planned for Q3 2027 and the company wants a
policy seat in the EU carbon-market reform conversation. The comms lead and
CFO join the 45-minute scorecard session, as the scorecard instructs.

## Step 1: The Go/No-Go Scorecard

| # | Factor | Weight | Score | Weighted | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Strategic visibility goals | x5 | 4 | 20 | Two live, named objectives (Series D raise, EU policy position) depend on audiences that gather that week. Not a 5: neither objective depends on that week alone; both have other viable paths |
| 2 | Audience fit | x4 | 3 | 12 | The right categories attend (climate-capital investors, EU policy figures, sustainability press). But of a draft top-20 list, warm paths exist to only 4 names today, below the one-third bar for a 5 |
| 3 | Story readiness | x4 | 3 | 12 | A defensible point of view ("settlement integrity is the missing layer of carbon markets") exists internally but is barely published: one byline, no anchor report. Three to six months of development needed |
| 4 | Budget reality | x3 | 4 | 12 | The calculator's promenade-only range (Step 2) fits inside the existing annual events budget without cutting the analyst-relations program that currently produces pipeline. Not a 5: it consumes the full contingency |
| 5 | Calendar cost | x2 | 4 | 8 | January 2027 is protectable now and Q4 2026 prep time can be booked. Not a 5: the raise process may compress December |
| 6 | Alternatives comparison | x2 | 3 | 6 | A self-hosted executive dinner series in Brussels plus one major climate-finance event is comparable for the policy goal at roughly half the cost. Davos wins only on investor concentration |

**Total: 70 of 100 → Conditional go.**

Per the scorecard rule, the lowest-scoring high-weight factors are fixed
first and the team re-scores in 60 days. Here that is factor 2 (warm paths)
and factor 3 (story readiness), exactly the pair the scorecard flags as the
usual culprits.

### The one-page recommendation (five lines)

1. **Score:** 70 of 100. Conditional go for Davos January 2027.
2. **Drivers:** strong named objectives (20/25) undercut by thin warm paths (12/20) and an unpublished point of view (12/20).
3. **Decision:** do not commit spend yet. Run the 90-day fix plan below, re-score in 60 days; commit only at 75 or above.
4. **What would change it:** warm paths to 7 or more of the top 20, and the anchor report published with early pickup.
5. **Budget implication:** reserve, do not spend, a planning envelope of $15,000 to $58,000 (promenade-only scenario, two people); release it only on a Go re-score.

Principal (CEO) plus comms lead, 5 nights, no badge, no hosted moment in
this scenario. All figures are public-range estimates as of the 2026 cycle.

| Line | Low | High | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Side-event access | $0 | $6,000 | Target: two climate-finance houses and one policy dinner; most are free with the right invitation |
| Lodging (2 people, 5 nights) | $8,000 | $22,000 | Davos-village high end vs down-valley low end |
| Travel | $3,500 | $9,000 | Amsterdam to Zurich x2 plus transfers |
| Ground logistics | $700 | $2,500 | Local transport, winter gear, contingency |
| Hosted moments | $0 | $0 | None in this scenario |
| Content and design | $2,000 | $8,000 | Briefing docs, one-pagers, talk track, social assets |
| PR and advance support | $800 | $10,000 | Meeting brokering and media outreach, internal time at the low end |
| **Total** | **$15,000** | **$57,500** | Within the kit's published promenade-only band of $15,000 to $40,000 at the lean end; the high end reflects peak lodging and full agency support |

Hidden time line: 4 principal days plus ~12 team days of preparation. At a
loaded executive-day value of $5,000, preparation adds a real internal cost
near $30,000. That number goes in the one-page recommendation, not under
the rug.

Carried to scorecard factor 4: the $15,000 to $58,000 envelope fits the
existing budget without cannibalizing a working program → score 4.

## Step 3: The runway, condensed to Solvra's next 90 days

The full kit runway is twelve months. A conditional go compresses the first
three phases into the 60-day fix window plus a 30-day buffer:

- **Days 1 to 15 (decide and position):** circulate the one-page recommendation; name the single point of view ("settlement integrity is the missing layer of carbon markets"); audit Maren's public footprint (one byline, two podcasts, no anchor report: the gap is the report)
- **Days 16 to 45 (publish and map):** commission the anchor report with a named analyst; place a flagship byline; build the 40-to-60-person target list; map last cycle's side-event landscape for climate-finance houses
- **Days 46 to 75 (build access):** convert the target list to warm paths through the board and Series C investors; goal is 7+ warm paths into the top 20; open conversations with the two or three best-fit side-event hosts
- **Days 76 to 90 (re-score):** rerun the scorecard with fresh evidence. At 75+ move to the full runway (book lodging immediately; July pricing beats October pricing). Below 75, redirect the envelope to the Brussels dinner series identified in factor 6

## Step 4: One meeting-request script, instantiated

Warm-introduction template from the kit, filled in for one fictional target
(a climate-fund partner, reached through a fictional board member):

> Subject: Intro to Dr. Elin Sørheim ahead of January?
>
> Pieter, I will be in Davos the week of January 18 and Dr. Sørheim is at
> the top of my list. We are both working on carbon-market settlement
> integrity; I published our "Missing Layer" report on exactly this. Would
> you be open to a two-line introduction? Happy to send you the note to
> forward.

Note what the template forced: the ask waited until the anchor report
existed to reference. Script quality is downstream of runway discipline,
which is the kit's core argument.

## What this example proves

- The scorecard produces a defensible, non-flattering answer (a conditional
  go, not the reflexive yes the buyer wanted)
- The calculator turns "roughly fifty grand?" into a lined, carryable range
  that feeds factor 4 honestly
- The runway converts the verdict into dated work, and the templates only
  fire once the runway has produced the assets they reference
- Every output above can be handed to a board without edits
