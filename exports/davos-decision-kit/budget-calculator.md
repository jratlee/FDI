# The Budget Calculator

Price the week before you commit to it. Fill in each line with the low and
high estimate, then carry the totals to the Go/No-Go Scorecard budget factor.

All figures are public-range estimates as of the 2026 cycle; verify current
prices before budgeting. Ranges assume one principal plus one support staffer
for the full week. This is not affiliated with the World Economic Forum, and
badge and access categories are set entirely by the Forum and its partners.

## 1. Access

| Line | Notes | Low | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WEF badge or membership | Formal participation runs from tens of thousands to well into six figures depending on membership tier; many attendees skip the badge and work the promenade and side events instead | $0 | $250,000+ |
| Side-event access | Many are free with the right invitation; hosted dinners and forums may charge | $0 | $15,000 |

The biggest strategic decision in the budget is badge or no badge. A large
share of the value of the week happens outside the badged perimeter.

## 2. Presence

| Line | Notes | Low | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lodging (per person, 5 to 6 nights) | Davos hotel prices multiply during the week; nearby towns (Klosters and down-valley) cost less but add transit time | $3,000 | $25,000 |
| Travel | Flights to Zurich plus train or car transfer | $1,500 | $10,000 |
| Ground logistics | Local transport, gear for snow and ice, contingency | $500 | $2,500 |

## 3. Program

| Line | Notes | Low | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hosted moments | A dinner, breakfast, or panel you host: venue, catering, production | $0 | $50,000 |
| Content and design | Briefing materials, one-pagers, talk tracks, social and media assets | $2,000 | $15,000 |
| PR and advance support | Meeting brokering, media outreach, on-the-ground support (agency or internal time) | $0 | $40,000 |

## 4. The hidden line: time

| Line | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Principal preparation | Plan on 3 to 5 working days spread over the prior quarter |
| Team preparation | Meeting-stack building, briefing docs, logistics: 10 to 20 working days |
| Opportunity cost | What does not happen in January because of this week |

Assign a real internal cost to these days. If a loaded executive day is worth
$5,000 to your organization, preparation alone is a five-figure line.

## 5. Totals

| Scenario | Typical all-in range |
| --- | --- |
| Promenade-only, lean (no badge, side events, tight lodging) | $15,000 to $40,000 |
| Promenade plus a hosted moment | $40,000 to $120,000 |
| Badged participation | $120,000 to $500,000+ |

## The rule of thumb

Write down the total, then write down what the same money buys elsewhere: a
year-long thought-leadership program, two owned executive dinner series, a
major industry-event presence. If Davos still wins on audience concentration
for your specific goals, the Scorecard will show it.
