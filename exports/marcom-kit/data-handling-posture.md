# Data Handling Posture: FDI MarCom OS
### What we do and do not do with client and prospect material

**False Dawn Industries. As of 2026. This posture governs the kit's hosted services (readiness scoring, the Process Defragmentation Report) and all advisory engagements.**

A product whose pitch is governance gets judged by its own governance. This is the standard we hold ourselves to, stated plainly so it can be quoted in a sales conversation or pasted into a vendor questionnaire.

## The commitments

1. **No training on client data.** Documents, workflow descriptions, and any material a client or prospect submits are used solely to produce the deliverable requested (a report, a score, an audit). They are never used to train models, never pooled across clients, and never used as examples for anyone else. One client per context, no exceptions.
2. **Minimal collection.** We ask for the minimum material needed for the deliverable. For a Process Defragmentation Report that is a workflow description, not a document dump.
3. **Defined retention.** Submitted material and generated reports are retained for 90 days after delivery (so the client can re-request the deliverable), then deleted. Advisory engagement materials are retained for the term of the engagement plus 90 days unless the contract states otherwise.
4. **Deletion on request.** Any client or prospect can request deletion of their submitted material and generated reports at any time; deletion is completed within 30 days and confirmed. This mirrors the self-serve erasure machinery already running on the FDI waitlist (per-record deletion, no residual copies, requests never logged with content).
5. **Hostile-input discipline.** Submitted documents are processed as data, never as instructions (prompt-injection defenses on every ingestion path). Outputs are sanitized before delivery.
6. **No resale, no sharing.** Client material is never sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties except processors strictly necessary to produce the deliverable (e.g. the model API), each bound by terms that prohibit training on the data.
7. **Confidentiality by default.** Client names and engagement details are not used in marketing without written permission.

## What this is not

This posture is an operating commitment, not a compliance certification and not legal advice. Clients in regulated industries should map these commitments against their own obligations with qualified counsel; we will answer any vendor-diligence questionnaire honestly and promptly.

## Where this appears

- At the point of submission on any hosted intake (readiness scoring, defrag report).
- In the Tier 3 engagement agreement.
- On request, as this standalone document.
