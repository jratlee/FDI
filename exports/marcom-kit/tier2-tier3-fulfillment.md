# Tier 2 and Tier 3 Fulfillment Outlines
### Living Engine, Transformation Sprint, and the Governance Risk Audit bridge

**FDI Agentic MarCom Architecture Kit. Internal fulfillment doc, not customer-facing copy. As of 2026.**

These are structural outlines, not full builds. Tier 1 (the Foundation Playbook) ships complete; the tiers below are defined here so pricing, checkout, and sales assets can reference exact scope, and so the founder knows what each sale commits.

---

## Tier 2: Living Engine ($199/mo, founding $149/mo, $1,500/yr annual, Agency Team $399/mo)

Positioning: the Foundation Playbook, kept alive. Frozen V1.0 answers rot as platforms, answer engines, and disclosure rules change; the subscription is the maintenance contract for the banks.

**Recurring deliverables (monthly cadence):**
1. **Guardrail updates.** Refreshed Riverbank constraint blocks and gate checklists as platforms and answer engines change behavior (e.g. new GEO retrieval constraints, new disclosure rules). Shipped as versioned markdown plus changelog.
2. **Machine-readable policy-as-code snippets.** The same rules in structured form for enforcement at the API gateway or via SkillFoundry's gates. (Delivery format matures alongside the SkillFoundry plugin; V1 is JSON conforming to the SkillFoundry audit-contract shape.)
3. **Agency Process Readiness Scoring.** Access to the hosted scoring backend: submit a workflow description, receive a scored readiness report (the productized sibling of the Process Defragmentation Report; see the generator build task). Fair-use quota per seat.
4. **Quarterly matrix refresh.** Updated Use, Compose, Build verdicts as the vendor landscape moves.

**Agency Team tier ($399/mo) adds:** multi-seat access and **white-label rights** to the Use, Compose, Build matrix in client pitches. White-label rights exist ONLY at this tier and above; the standard $199 tier is internal-use licensed.

**Ops commitments:** monthly update cadence (calendar-blocked), changelog discipline, deprecation notices one cycle ahead. Churn risk is silence; the update email IS the product.

## Tier 3: Agentic Agency Transformation Sprint ($5,000/mo retainer, max ~3 active, or $10,000 fixed sprint)

**The sprint is fixed at four weeks, fixed scope.** This removes the retainer-vs-sprint arbitrage: the $10k sprint is a four-week engagement; the $5k/mo retainer is the ongoing advisory relationship after (or instead of) a sprint.

**Four-week sprint structure:**
- **Week 1: Audit to Kill.** Pre-collected process inventory; half-day executive workshop; signed one-page kill list with 30-day dates. (Blueprint 04 run live.)
- **Week 2: Riverbank installation.** Client-specific constraint blocks for their top two workflows, installed in shadow mode; escalation trees agreed with their counsel. Custom-tuned guardrails for regulated clients are scoped here with counsel in the loop; FDI supplies architecture, never legal advice.
- **Week 3: Hourglass pod design.** Pilot pod staffed and chartered (3 to 5 senior expert generalists plus protected junior seat); gates flipped from shadow to enforcing; kill list executed.
- **Week 4: First full cycle plus handoff.** Pod runs one complete cycle; metrics baseline (hours returned, escalations, incidents); leadership readout; 90-day roadmap.

**Retainer ($5k/mo) includes:** the sprint cadence stretched across ongoing pods, a private async channel for architectural strategy on live pitches, and quarterly re-audits. Capacity is capped at roughly three active clients; scarcity is real and stated.

**Not included at any tier:** media buying, execution staffing, legal or compliance sign-off, guarantees of specific margin outcomes.

## The bridge: Governance Risk Audit ($1,500 to $2,500 one-time)

A standalone paid diagnostic that de-risks the Tier 3 decision (and creates cash earlier in the pipeline):
1. Client supplies their current QA/review chain for one high-volume workflow plus a sample of recent AI-assisted output.
2. FDI runs the output against the baseline Riverbank gates and the review chain against the Audit to Kill tests.
3. Deliverable: a branded report showing where the current QA layer cannot keep pace with generation speed, the top five kill candidates, and a scoped sprint recommendation.
4. 100 percent of the audit fee credits against a Transformation Sprint signed within 60 days.

Fulfillment leans on the Process Defragmentation Report generator once built; V1 can be produced manually from the playbook in roughly half a day.
