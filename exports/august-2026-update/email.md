The caveat at the bottom of v1 said "the formula does not account for joy." Users kept pointing at it. The caveat became the feature.

**The thing I keep thinking about.**

Every project this month confirmed the same thing from a different angle: AI didn't lower the bar for what's worth building. It raised the cost of not knowing.

The bottleneck used to be execution — could you write it, build it, ship it? That's solved. The bottleneck now is judgment — do you know what's true, what's scarce, what's worth your reader's time? The agentic web will make this legible in Shapley math and crawl prices. The preparation is the same as it's always been: build something worth finding.

The Field Guide series is the map. The Engine is the first tool for the terrain.
