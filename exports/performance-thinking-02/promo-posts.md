## Post 1 · The archetype (launch day)

The most important marketing hire of the next decade is not on your org chart yet.

We call the role the Forward-Deployed Communications Professional. Borrowed from engineering: one operator who owns an end-to-end workflow, runs AI agents as on-demand domain experts, and applies the two things agents cannot supply, strategy and taste.

Four traits to filter for, none of which are certifications:
1. First-principles thinking, because the playbooks expired.
2. Systems curiosity, because multi-agent workflows fail in new ways.
3. High-fidelity taste, because technically correct and aesthetically dead is the default AI output.
4. Orchestration, of people and of agents.

## Post 2 · The machine buyer (day 2)

An AI purchasing agent never sees your homepage.

It reads your structured data. It checks your service terms. It queries your verifiable transaction history. If anything is vague or fragmented, it does not ask a clarifying question. It routes the purchase to a competitor whose data parses cleanly.

That is what marketing to machines means, and research on agent-to-agent economies (AgentCards, ledger-anchored identity) says it is arriving faster than most teams think.

Machine legibility is now a brand asset with a balance. We mapped the whole shift: falsedawn.industries/fdcp

## Post 3 · The trust wall (day 4)

Two numbers from the Financial Times study New Dimensions of Influence:

74 percent of business leaders have delayed or killed decisions because they did not know what data to trust.
82 percent trust a source that challenges their assumptions more than one that confirms them.

Free content made attention cheap and trust expensive. The response is not more content. It is the Proof Stack: deployed integrations, hard data, organic traction, and independent validation, in that order, before a single word of roadmap.

## Post 4 · The loops (day 6)

Manual prompting is artisanal work. The leverage is in loops.

A verification loop scores every output against brand, legal, and accuracy rubrics before anything ships. An SEO/GEO loop audits your AI citation share continuously, with automated rollback. An ads loop iterates creative against return on ad spend for pennies per run.

The operator does not sit inside the loops. They engineer the loops, then spend human attention on the two inputs loops cannot generate: strategy and taste.
