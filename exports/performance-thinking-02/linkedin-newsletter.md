# LinkedIn newsletter edition · Performance Thinking 02

**Edition title:** The Forward-Deployed Communicator

**Hook (first line, under 110 characters):**
Your next biggest buyer will be a machine. Most marketing org charts still assume it has eyeballs.

---

**Body:**

For decades the MarCom department was an assembly line: strategist to copywriter to designer to media buyer. It existed to manage one constraint, the cost and speed of human execution. Agentic AI deleted that constraint. Content is now effectively free, and the new bottlenecks are verification, alignment, security, and taste.

Meanwhile the buyer itself is changing. Analysts tracking agentic commerce expect trillions in B2B purchasing to become agent-mediated within a few years. An AI purchasing agent never sees your landing page. It reads your structured data, checks your verifiable track record, and routes the order in milliseconds.

The operator built for this market is what we call the Forward-Deployed Communications Professional, the FDCP. Borrowed from engineering's forward-deployed model: one person who owns an end-to-end workflow, runs a fleet of AI agents as on-demand experts, and stands at the exact point where rising human skepticism meets machine discoverability.

In [the full report](https://falsedawn.industries/fdcp) we map the whole system:

- The four hiring traits that outlast any certification: first-principles thinking, systems curiosity, high-fidelity taste, and orchestration of both people and agents.
- The Hourglass org shape: protect your 2034 senior pipeline, then arm the junior base with agents to expand margin and customer surface area while the Diamond trap quietly eats both.
- Loop Engineering: verification, SEO/GEO, and ads-iteration loops that run for pennies while humans keep strategy and taste.
- Policy as Code, the Riverbank: brand and budget rules compiled into infrastructure so autonomous speed stays safe.
- The Proof Stack and the FT's Four Dimensions of Influence: receipts before roadmap for an audience that no longer trusts claims.
- The pivot from SEO to GEO/LEO, and marketing to machines via structured data and AgentCards.

Every claim is sourced by name: AWS, the Financial Times, Gartner via Ramp, Anthropic's Fiona Fung, LangChain, Addy Osmani, a16z crypto, and the AgentCards research team.
