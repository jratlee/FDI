The next three Field Guides · Market mechanics
Three markets, three new forms brand identity has to take
01
Aggregated
A few models mediate discovery
Mechanic
The model is your first audience. It summarizes, ranks, and answers for you, so the customer meets a machine's version of you first.
Brand's new form
A brand legible to machines: one consistent, structured point of view the model can retrieve and repeat without distorting you.
02
Decentralized
Attention fragments across communities
Mechanic
Reach gives way to belonging. No single channel holds your audience, so trust is earned in many small rooms you don't control.
Brand's new form
A portable identity system: voice, symbols, and POV modular enough to stay recognizably you as other people carry it.
03
Autonomous
Agents transact on everyone's behalf
Mechanic
The buyer becomes software. Agents choose by verifiable criteria and clean interfaces, not by emotion or creative, then act.
Brand's new form
Trust rendered as code: proofs, credentials, and a safe interface an agent can check and confidently prefer.
Culture earns the belief. Code makes it legible, portable, and verifiable.
