Field Guide 003 · Decentralized markets

Rented platform identity vs. owned onchain identity.

Web2 / platform identity
Stored in a company's database
Portable?
No. Lives on one platform.
Revocable?
Yes — by platform policy.
Verifiable?
Only by the platform.
Composable?
Locked behind APIs.
Agent-readable?
Requires OAuth + rate limits.
Onchain identity · wallet + ENS + EAS
Controlled by the wallet holder
Portable?
Yes — same across all EVM protocols.
Revocable?
No company can revoke it.
Verifiable?
By any party, publicly, on-chain.
Composable?
Attestations stack across protocols.
Agent-readable?
Yes — direct on-chain query.
