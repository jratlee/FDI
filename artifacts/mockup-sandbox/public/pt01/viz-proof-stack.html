The Proof Stack: receipts before vision
The old ratio
80 / 20
vision to substance, and it worked
The new ratio
Proof first
vision must be earned by the substance under it
01
Partnerships with teeth
Deployed integrations with partners willing to validate them publicly. MOUs and "collaborations" carry zero weight.
02
Hard data
Active users, retention curves, transaction volume. Numbers that survive independent third-party scrutiny.
03
Unmanufactured pull
Organic community growth that predates the PR push. Retained behavior that signals genuine market pull.
04
Third-party validation
Unsolicited coverage, independent research, and audits nobody paid for. The trust signals you cannot buy.
1,000 engaged, retained users beat a hypothetical million. Lead with the most concrete number you have.
