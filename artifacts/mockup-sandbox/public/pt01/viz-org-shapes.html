Performance Thinking · People
Four shapes, one survivor: the Hourglass
01
The Pyramid
Legacy · too slow
Many juniors execute manual work under a few seniors. Built for a world where human throughput was the constraint. It isn't anymore.
02
The Diamond
The trap · critical risk
Cut the junior base, bulk up the middle to watch the AI. Looks efficient on a spreadsheet. Destroys the learning pipeline entirely.
03
The Inverted Pyramid
Fast now · fatal later
Pods of 3 to 5 senior generalists running AI. Maximum output today, zero pathway for anyone to become senior tomorrow.
04
The Hourglass
Optimal · protects 2034
Senior pods execute at the top, the middle thins out, and a protected junior base learns at the bottom. Their output is judgment, not assets.
Expert judgment is cultivated, not hired. No junior base today means no senior bench in 2034.
