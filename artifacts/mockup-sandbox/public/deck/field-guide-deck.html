The flood has a second-order effect
Disillusionomics
/ dɪs·ɪˌluː·ʒənˈɒm·ɪks / noun

When the machinery of persuasion becomes visible, consumers price it in. Every tactic they can see is a tactic they discount — and baseline trust in marketing resets lower.

Term coined by economist Alice Lassman · popularized via Fortune, Jan 2026

TPL/LIST · 11/13
The CMO action plan

Six imperatives for 2026

01

Rebalance toward retention

Acquisition-only budgets refill a leaking map. Fund structural retention before the cohort is gone.

02

Adopt Shapley-value attribution

Last-click is structurally gameable. Credit the whole journey with game-theoretic attribution.

03

Set rational constraints

Impossible ROAS targets don't push algorithms harder — they trigger clipping and throttle delivery.

04

Make AI use visibly ethical

Transparency is now brand equity. Private, provable data handling beats clever targeting.

05

Preserve market thickness

Hyper-granular targeting collapses reach and distorts price floors. Target culture, not microdots.

06

Treat identity as infrastructure

No reactive pivots. One coherent self across every touchpoint, platform, and year.
