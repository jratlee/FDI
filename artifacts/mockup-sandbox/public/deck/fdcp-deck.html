False Dawn Industries
Process · 07/12
Loop engineering · after LangChain and Addy Osmani

The FDCP does not sit in the loops. They engineer them.

GATEWAY

Verification loop

Every output scored against brand, legal, and accuracy rubrics before anything ships. Fail means retry with feedback. A human signs off at the end.

DISCOVERY

SEO / GEO loop

Continuously audits properties, monitors AI citation frequency, and tests structures, with automated rollback so no silent damage.

SPEND

Ads iteration loop

Generates variants, watches return on ad spend, adjusts until profitable, at compute cost that is a rounding error next to a retainer.
