# /brief

Generate a full Top Call intelligence brief from the corpus.

Runs the `brief-generation` skill: the Top Call, TLDR, executive moves, bottom
line, and a source-authority log — every line provenance-stamped. Optionally
scope to one `category`.

- MCP tool: `generate_brief`
- CLI: `node bin/topcall.mjs brief [--category Y]`
