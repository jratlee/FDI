## Why this matters to FDI

FDI's core thesis — owned marketing systems compound while rented reach decays — is
validated and operationalized by the Ghost content distribution framework. The
framework gives FDI three things:

1. **A language layer**: the three-channel taxonomy (Owned / Earned / Paid) is the
   vocabulary FDI uses with clients to explain why their current stack is fragile.
2. **A strategy scaffold**: the six-part Steph Smith model maps directly onto FDI's
   own channels and onto the systems FDI builds for clients.
3. **An execution discipline**: the 1:1 creation:distribution rule governs how FDI
   ships its own content, and is what MarCom OS teaches clients to implement.

## The three distribution channels

| Channel | What it is | Time horizon | FDI application |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Owned** | You control it: SEO, email list, your own social profiles, your site | Long — slow to build, durable forever | The entire FDI thesis. The Field Guide corpus, the email list, llms.txt for AI citation, the MarCom OS Riverbank. |
| **Earned / Shared** | Other people's audiences: mentions, shares, reviews, guest posts | Medium — relationship-dependent | Citations in AI answers, LinkedIn shares of Field Guides, Tier 1 referrals into Tier 2 accounts. SkillFoundry's Algorithmic Signal gate measures whether content earns this. |
| **Paid** | Costs money: ads, sponsored content, newsletter sponsorships | Short — fast but not durable without owned foundation | Not FDI's primary play. Appropriate only once owned + earned channels are established. |

> **The key insight FDI leads with to clients:** Paid channels bring faster results
> but cost money continuously. Owned and earned channels take longer but are
> sustainable — they compound rather than reset when you stop paying.

## Steph Smith's six-part distribution model — mapped to FDI

| Layer | What it is | FDI channel / asset |
|---|---|---|
| **Bedrock** | Reliable channels that take long to build (SEO, email) | Field Guide series (public, indexable) · Email list (double opt-in waitlist) · llms.txt (AI citation layer) · `/sitemap.xml` |
| **Viral** | Low probability, very high return (Reddit, HN) | Occasional Field Guide posts on Hacker News / relevant subreddits when a new guide ships |
| **Social** | Community-building on social platforms | LinkedIn (primary + only committed platform) · LinkedIn articles (syndicated from Field Guides) |
| **Syndication** | Repost or repurpose existing content elsewhere | Field Guide article → LinkedIn long-form · Field Guide → quote cards → X posts · Deck PDF as SlideShare / LinkedIn document post |
| **Targeted** | Non-scalable: DM potential fans and influencers | Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3 cold-email sequences · Direct LinkedIn outreach to named accounts |
| **Paid** | Spend money to reach others | Deferred — not in current FDI budget cycle |

## The 1:1 creation:distribution rule

> "Ideally, a creator should be dedicating the **same amount of time** and effort to
> distribution as they do to creation from day one. For every hour you spend
> writing, researching, filming, or publishing, you should also spend an hour
> sharing, mastering a social platform, starting conversations in digital
> communities, and reaching out to new potential fans."
> — Ghost Resources, *Content Distribution*

**FDI interpretation:** Every Field Guide shipped needs an equal distribution
effort. A guide written but not distributed is a thesis asserted, not proven. The
current ratio for FDI is too creation-heavy — this is the single biggest lever on
reach.

### Distribution checklist for every FDI Field Guide

- [ ] LinkedIn long-form article (synthesized from the guide, not a copy-paste)
- [ ] Social copy: 3–5 LinkedIn posts pulling individual insights
- [ ] X/Twitter thread version (same insights, shorter format)
- [ ] Email to waitlist (personal tone, links to both the guide and the LinkedIn article)
- [ ] Direct outreach to 5–10 named Tier 1 contacts (targeted, non-scalable)
- [ ] One community post (relevant LinkedIn group, Slack, or forum — only where appropriate)
- [ ] Deck PDF as a LinkedIn document post (drives high organic reach on LinkedIn)

### Distribution checklist for every major MarCom OS / SkillFoundry update

- [ ] Changelog note to email list
- [ ] LinkedIn post (framed as product news, not a press release)
- [ ] Update the `/roadmap` page so the site reflects the change

## The four-step sustainable distribution strategy

Adapted from Ghost's recommended path for new creators — applied to FDI's current
growth stage (Field Guides 001–004 live, email list building, LinkedIn as primary
social channel):

1. **SEO — the bedrock**: Field Guides are public, long-form, and indexed. The
   `/sitemap.xml` is honest (correct `lastmod`). `llms.txt` extends this to AI
   citation. Every new field guide extends the compounding SEO surface. Do not skip
   on-page optimization (title tags, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, JSON-LD
   already in place).

2. **Community — the unscalable edge**: Tier 1 outreach IS this step. Direct
   replies to LinkedIn comments, one-on-one conversations with named CMOs, help
   offered before a pitch is made. The Tier 1 sequencing (Task #177 and related)
   is the community-building investment, not a pipeline task.

3. **Social — one platform, mastered**: LinkedIn is FDI's committed platform.
   The rule: do not spread to Instagram, X, TikTok simultaneously. Master
   LinkedIn's algorithm (document posts, articles, and native video outperform
   link posts) and stay consistent. LinkedIn article format rules already in the
   growth skill.

4. **Syndication — multiply the surface area**: Every field guide should be
   repurposed into at least three formats before it is considered "distributed":
   - Full article on the site (canonical)
   - LinkedIn article (adapted, not copy-pasted; canonical points back to site)
   - Quote cards / data cards for social
   - Deck PDF as a LinkedIn document post

## Key principles to carry forward

1. **Owned channels are the FDI thesis AND the FDI strategy.** FDI practices what
   it preaches: the site, the email list, and the Field Guide series are the
   owned-channel bedrock.

2. **Distribution is not a post-publish step — it is half the work.** The
   1:1 rule applies to every FDI content asset.

3. **One social platform, mastered.** LinkedIn only. Depth over breadth.

4. **Syndication multiplies surface area without creating new content.** Every
   guide has at least three legitimate distribution surfaces before it is
   exhausted.

5. **Targeted outreach (Tier 1/2/3 sequences) is the Steph Smith "Targeted"
   layer.** It is non-scalable by design and that is its strength — it is the
   fastest way to grow in the early stage.

6. **The bedrock takes time and does not reset.** Twelve months of consistent
   Field Guide publishing + email list building is worth more than a single viral
   LinkedIn post. Do not sacrifice the bedrock for short-term spikes.
